## Interpretation and limits

**Material sampling limitation:** all 16 later held-position sessions lacked an opening baseline. The fail-closed gate blocked new entries throughout those sessions, including 55 triggered evaluations. This is a diagnostic result for this acquisition method, not a representative full-strategy performance test. The first-minute data correction is registered separately as QUOTE-004C and does not overwrite this result.

Independent cash/share/fee/P&L reconstruction **passed**, checking 1343 frames and 1 fill. A second replay produced an identical result. All 17 complete input files are hashed; final tested replay sources and the original acquisition definition are retained separately.

This sample cannot establish a repeatable strategy edge. CRM/MSFT were previously researched and are a retrospective fixed universe, not reconstructed historical watchlist membership or a holdout. Historical earnings, listing/classification and per-symbol fractional permission are assumptions. Actual arrival latency, continuous book depth and overnight actions outside observed coverage are not established. Benchmark quote availability and any unavailable marks are explicit in the JSON report.

The original CRWD/DDOG attempt was rejected before quote acquisition because CRWD has an unsupported July 2026 split in its raw profile history. That failed protocol and its evidence remain separate. The replacement download retained one rate-limited partial attempt, then reacquired the whole affected session at a slower rate. No failed session enters these metrics.

Current research settings: 1% planned risk per trade, 20% position cap, eight positions, six decimal places, 5 bps adverse market slippage, 20-second quote freshness, 100% active allocation, 0% cash floor and the applied 20% daily-loss pause. These settings were read and frozen, not applied or changed.
