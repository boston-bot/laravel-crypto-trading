## Behavior

The independently default-off `MCP_SIGNAL_PAPER_HANDOFF_ENABLED` gate enables the new `SignalPaperHandoff` boundary. Only a stored deterministic strategy signal can enter it. It does not use AI decisions, target weights, model sizing or the legacy crypto executor. The existing superseded AI handoff is unchanged and stays disabled; selecting rule eligibility already blocks it.

An authenticated, CSRF-protected, account-scoped Control Center form explicitly applies an immutable signal execution policy. Reviewer identity comes from the session. The policy binds the current allocation and execution versions, stores a complete dated cost schedule, and defaults to 1% planned risk per trade, a 20% position cap, eight occupied positions (including pending buys), 1 bp adverse slippage, 5 bp limit offset and 20-second quote age. Bounds prohibit risk above 1%, position cap above 20% and more than eight positions. Fractional LIMIT execution remains unverified, so the form and service require whole shares. Applying a policy changes neither balances nor service flags; changing the underlying allocation/execution version requires a new policy application. Repeated request IDs are idempotent and changed inputs conflict.

For buys, quantity is rounded down under the planned-risk, position, cash-floor and sleeve-allocation caps. Fresh bid marks value held paper positions; unavailable marks block sizing. The price risk uses the opening signal's pinned ATR with its stop multiple, plus conservative estimates of execution costs and stop slippage. This is a planned stop-risk budget, not a guarantee against gaps. The ask determines a limit with the owner offset, capped by the playbook's maximum chase price. A zero whole-share quantity is recorded as `no_order`. With $500 paper equity and a 20% cap, any share above the $100 position budget is skipped until fractional support is verified or the owner separately changes capital/policy within supported bounds.

Submission and every later fill recheck policy identity, cost-schedule validity, signal/account provenance, current permissions, quote/classification/action coverage, eligibility/watchlist/external-order entry safety, profile/history/benchmark/earnings evidence, position and pending-order limits, cash and sleeve capacity, planned risk and concentration. Submission also checks the exact limit offset against fresh marks and prevents using a signal after position history changes. Only the original order's partial fills may complete a position; another order cannot add to it. Replaced policies or expired cost schedules release pending reservations through revalidation even with no usable quote.

Exit orders refer to the same actual paper episode as their signal. They size only the unreserved shares of the selected sleeve. A latched exit with an older unfilled SELL remainder cancels that remainder, releases its shares and creates a new immutable DAY limit intent for the new cycle. Entry membership, earnings entry filters, position budgets and the daily-loss baseline do not block exits; current data, accounting and execution permissions still apply. An unavailable mark or unresolved corporate action never invents a sell price.

`mcp:signal-paper-cycle --once [--account=<local-id>]` first processes existing signal-linked orders using later stored observations, then records/reuses the current signal cycle and performs idempotent handoffs. The combined five-minute schedule requires signal evaluation, signal handoff and real-data paper fills all enabled. The standalone signal schedule is suppressed when handoff is selected. No observation collection, model request, broker submission or cancellation is performed. Repeating a cycle cannot create duplicate orders or fills; a signal's terminal handoff outcome cannot turn into another order on retry. Old synthetic/model orders are not automatically processed by this schedule.

## Costs and reviewed sources

The owner-applied `rh_listed_equity_2026q4_v1` schedule stores rates, thresholds, rounding, sources and validity dates in PostgreSQL; each new signal order pins that complete snapshot in its immutable assumptions. Rates are not environment constants and no network fee lookup runs in the loop. The review is bounded through December 31, 2026; January requires another reviewed version rather than guessing.

The [Robinhood fee rules](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood/) specify $0 listed-equity commissions, sell-only SEC fees of $20.60 per million with a $500-or-less waiver and upward cent rounding, and no TAF during October–December 2026. They also list CAT at $0.000003 per listed share on both sides, dropping charges below a cent and otherwise rounding to the nearest cent. The simulator applies these rules per simulated execution. Each later displayed-book fill is modeled as one execution; actual broker execution grouping remains unverified. Buy reservations allow for partial-execution rounding, with unused cash released on fills/cancellation. Legacy paper fee assumptions and historical fills remain unchanged.

The [SEC advisory](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/fee-rate-advisories/2026-2) confirms the Section 31 rate, and its [advisory index](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/fee-rate-advisories) identified it as the latest Section 31 advisory at review. The [FINRA filing](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rule-filings/sr-finra-2026-021) and [SEC notice](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/finra/2026/34-106409.pdf) document the temporary TAF pause. Non-equity, OTC, ADR custody, options and live execution costs are outside this schedule's supported scope.

## Persistence and migration impact

Migration `2026_10_19_000024_create_mcp_signal_execution.php` adds encrypted immutable `mcp_signal_execution_policies` and `mcp_signal_order_handoffs`, with unique owner-version/request and per-signal handoff keys. Signal intents have stable UUIDs derived from the signal ID, retain their strategy signal/policy references, and use the existing separate paper order/event/cash ledger. The handoff, intent and reservations commit in the same connection/portfolio transaction; a crash after intent creation rolls everything back for a safe retry.

The same migration widens three paper-order timestamps (`accepted_at`, `expires_at`, `last_source_at`) and paper-event `recorded_at` to six fractional digits, with matching model serialization. Otherwise a newly recorded microsecond signal could appear later than its truncated paper acceptance time. Existing values are preserved; no backfill invents source precision. SQLite expiry lookup now uses matching precision at the exact expiry boundary. Rolling back the new tables intentionally leaves the compatible timestamp widening intact.

No operator database was migrated. Deployment from runtime `abb8461` still needs both the preceding `000023` signal migration and this `000024` migration: four new evidence tables in total, plus these timestamp changes. Use the existing database/schema identity checks, backup and separate owner migration workflow; the restricted runtime login cannot perform DDL.
