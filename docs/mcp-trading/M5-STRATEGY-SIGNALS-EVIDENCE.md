# M5 deterministic strategy signals

October 19, 2026. Source implementation only; not deployed, migrated or enabled in runtime. M5 remains partial and M6 unstarted. This increment records paper strategy evidence; it creates no orders, fills, reservations or brokerage mutations and makes no model calls.

## Delivered behavior

`MCP_STRATEGY_SIGNALS_ENABLED=false` independently defaults the stored-data runner off. When enabled, `mcp:strategy-cycle --once [--account=<local-connection-id>]` records one account's current five-minute cycle, or processes configured operator accounts when omitted. Laravel schedules it every five minutes only behind this flag. There is no network collection inside the cycle. Late/missing observations remain unavailable; a schedule is not a guarantee that the existing bounded collector covers the full universe on time.

Entries use the current nominating watchlists, full rule eligibility, a complete current-authorization account snapshot less than five minutes old, current order coverage, a profile matched to the latest stored symbol and SPY history and latest completed exchange session, fresh bid/ask components, a current earnings check and the SPY filter. Data-only members are evaluated for audit but cannot generate entries. Breakout and pullback require ask strictly above the playbook trigger and at most 0.5 opening-profile ATR above it. The first 15 minutes after the verified open block entries; the exact 15-minute boundary is allowed. An existing position in any paper sleeve or broker holdings prevents another entry. Open paper orders and a five-trading-session cooldown after a flattening fill also block entry. Eligibility price/spread/liquidity rules remain independent prerequisites. No risk size or permission to submit an order is inferred from a BUY record.

Exits apply only to paper positions with a reconstructible fill history and an opening order linked to this engine's immutable BUY signal via `intent.strategy_signal_id`. The future signal handoff must persist that link; this change does not retrofit the superseded model handoff. Legacy/synthetic or broker holdings without that strategy entry context produce an explicit unavailable result, with no invented entry price, stop or adoption of live holdings. Current positions with accounting/review blocks remain unavailable. A changed share quantity not explained by the fill history also fails closed pending an action-aware position-context implementation.

For a supported paper episode, actual opening fills determine the weighted entry price, excluding fees; the opening signal pins ATR and parameter values. The initial stop is entry minus 2 ATR. The trailing stop is the highest completed close since entry minus 3 opening ATR, floored by the initial stop and every previously recorded trailing level for that episode. Opening ATR is deliberately fixed for the episode in this rule version, making stops reproducible and preventing volatility changes from widening them. Other exits are a completed close below the 50-session SMA, 20 completed sessions without any close reaching entry plus one opening ATR, and the last session before a known report (today or the next trading session, conservatively including tentative/unknown timing). Once triggered, an episode's exit remains latched across cycles and restarts until the position is closed. No repricing or order creation occurs yet.

Held exit evaluation does not depend on watchlist freshness/membership, entry eligibility enablement, the SPY filter or external-order entry pauses. Fresh scoped quotes, regular exchange hours and corporate-action clearance still apply. Known stop levels can trigger exits even with missing daily/earnings reference data; absent facts never manufacture a reference-data exit. Unknown corporate actions block comparison of potentially incompatible prices and stops. An unknown calendar blocks evaluation; a verified closed session records a wait.

## Persistence and visibility

Additive migration `2026_10_19_000023_create_mcp_strategy_signals.php` creates `mcp_strategy_cycles` and `mcp_strategy_signals` in the configured schema. No backfill. Cycle identity includes connection, bound-account scope, authorization generation, rule version and five-minute slot. A connection lock then a portfolio lock serialize snapshots of mutable state with existing paper accounting. All symbol outcomes commit atomically; a crash rolls back the cycle. Duplicate workers/restarts return the existing immutable outcome set, including an empty cycle, instead of adding signals or revising an earlier unavailable result in the same slot.

Each immutable encrypted outcome retains rule/parameter versions, watchlist revision, profile and fingerprint, market input identity/source/retrieval/recording times, account/order-read evidence, ledger/execution versions, session evidence, position/fill facts, current eligibility/earnings evidence, result, cutoff and a SHA-256 input fingerprint. Cutoff is captured after acquiring locks; there is no historical-cutoff interface for mutable current positions. PostgreSQL constraints reject signal/side combinations outside the supported states. The authenticated, account-scoped Control Center exposes paginated signal/wait/unavailable history and detailed evidence. It describes the external ChatGPT strategy split and keeps superseded AI decisions disabled.
