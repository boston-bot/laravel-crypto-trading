No alternate strategy engine or execution gateway is introduced.

A market order may fill against its own admitted submission quote, matching the forward paper timing convention. Remainders require a later source observation. Limit orders still require observations after acceptance. Both paths enforce displayed share quantities, consume each symbol/side/source's liquidity once, recheck entries, preserve unknown marks, expire DAY remainders and carry positions chronologically. Quote, multi-session and experiment replay versions advance to v2; daily replay remains v3. Default acquisition now also records a data-only observation 30 seconds after the open. A missing exact-open baseline can recover inside the first minute only while no order or fill has changed accounting; late observations remain unavailable, and exits remain independent. The valuation frame creates no signals or fills. The data contract advances to `historical_sip_raw_regular_5m_opening30s_full_sessions_v2`, so old datasets cannot be silently reused as though they contained this observation.

Independent accounting verification v2 reconstructs fractional inventory, fees, cash, closed and marked P&L, quote-source chronology, shared liquidity and equity/drawdown. It rejects tampered precision, order type, future source/acceptance times and cash. Results expose execution mode; the experiment page labels price bounds and order types correctly. The frozen source set now includes shared price-bound and fractional-contract implementations. Experiment cost-date validation uses New York dates, fixing the October 1 boundary previously interpreted as September 30 at UTC midnight. Research definitions still cannot override execution permissions.

Historical fractional eligibility, earnings, listing and classification remain assumptions. Immediate sampled-quote fills with assumed slippage do not reproduce actual historical arrival latency, continuous book depth, queue priority or broker execution. No edge or forward-paper acceptance follows.
